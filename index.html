01 · About

            
Turning signals into systems you can trust.

            

              I’m an IT engineer specializing in embedded systems, with experience in automation,
              networking and system administration. I’m originally from Iraq and have lived in Finland since 2014, where I
              earned a Bachelor of Engineering in IT (Embedded Systems) at VAMK, on top of a degree in Pure Mathematics.
              I’m now studying for a Master of Science (Technology) in Information Security.
            

            

              I enjoy hard problems: designing control systems, building automated test rigs, or tuning a
              Bluetooth Low Energy mesh until every packet arrives. Math helps me reason about a problem, and the lab
              helps me prove the answer works.
